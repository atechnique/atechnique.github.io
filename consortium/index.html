📜 One Big Consortium: Charter (Version 0.1)

Verbatim Public Text · Founding Secretary: Christopher Vivian Duguay (Winnipeg)

Version 0.1. One page by design. Anyone may read it. There is nothing here to steal: the Consortium is the people who countersign, not the text.

What This Is

One Big Consortium is an unincorporated association of artists and patrons who form small syndicates, one per project, to make and sell work together under a written, deterministic split of the money. The Consortium owns nothing, holds no one’s rights, and binds no one beyond the project sheets they sign.

Membership

  1. Membership is by countersign. Any artist or patron joins the Consortium by signing this charter. There is no other admission process, no fee, and no approval committee.
  2. Exit is free and instant. A member leaves by saying so. Leaving cancels nothing already signed: project sheets already in force complete under their own terms.

Projects

  1. Work happens in project syndicates. Any members may form one for a single project by signing a Split Sheet naming the work, the roles, and the shares totaling 100 percent.
  2. The Split Sheet rules the project. Shares are fixed at signing, money divides exactly as written, costs are only those declared on the sheet, and every participant receives every receipt.
  3. A syndicate dissolves when its project’s payouts complete. It may re-form for a new project under a new sheet.

The Node

  1. The Consortium keeps a Secretary, the node, who holds copies of charters and sheets, routes information between members, and keeps the records findable. The founding Secretary is Christopher Vivian Duguay of Winnipeg. The Secretary’s power is the paperwork: the node keeps the records, the records decide disputes, and the node holds no share in any project by virtue of the office.
  2. No one may bind the whole Consortium. Only a project’s signatories are bound by that project’s sheet. There is no general fund, no common debt, and no officer who can sign for everyone.

Disputes and Revision

  1. Complaints are design input. Any member may file a complaint about any project or about this charter, in writing, to the node. Complaints are logged, answered from the paperwork where the paperwork speaks, and where it does not, the gap is written into the next version of the relevant document.
  2. This charter may be revised by a new version countersigned by the members it will govern. Old sheets are never revised retroactively.

The One Exclusion

  1. The Consortium accommodates every kind of participant and every kind of project except one: work whose terms require lying about the split, the receipts, or the provenance of the work. Such projects cannot be sheeted. Everything else is negotiable in the open, on the paper, where everyone can see it.
