## Terms

1. **Deterministic split.** When money arrives for the work named above, it is divided exactly by the percentages on this sheet. No participant may renegotiate a share after the money arrives.
2. **Costs first, declared in advance.** Only costs listed here are taken off the top before the split: ______________________________. Costs not listed here are borne by whoever incurred them.
3. **Receipts are public inside the project.** Every participant receives the invoice, the payment record, and the split calculation for every payout. The receipt is the record. If it is not in the paperwork, it did not happen.
4. **Resale and reuse.** If the work is sold again, licensed, or reused in a new project, this sheet applies to the original work's share of the new money unless a new sheet names a different arrangement before the new money arrives.
5. **Patron syndicates.** A patron participant's share represents their contribution as agreed in the Role column. Patrons syndicate under the same rules as artists: shares fixed at signing, receipts shared, exit free after the project's payout completes.
6. **Disputes.** A participant who believes a split was executed wrongly says so in writing to every other participant. The paperwork decides. If the paperwork genuinely does not answer the question, the participants vote by share, and the question is written into the next sheet so it cannot recur unanswered.
7. **No lock on the person.** Signing this sheet binds the signer to this project only. It creates no claim on any other project, past or future.
