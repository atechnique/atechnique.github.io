🛠️ 3. The 7 Weapons of Reality Auditing (Plain-English Cheat Sheet)

Every Technique Inside PARHDM Explained Without Academic Gatekeeping
Weapon 01 · Bivariate Signal

Pearson r vs. Spearman Rank ρ

Range: −1.00 to +1.00 (0 = No Link)
Plain English: Pearson r checks if two variables move along a straight ruler line. Spearman ρ converts numbers into 1st-through-50th place ranks first so one extreme billionaire/island state can't hijack the score.
How Bad Takes Happen: Reporting only r when a single extreme outlier creates a fake line—or hiding r² (the % of variation explained: r = 0.80 means 0.80² = 64%!).
Dossier #001: Religion 'Very Important' hit ρ = −0.809 & Secular 'Nones' hit r = +0.804 (R² = 64.6% alone).
Weapon 02 · Multiple-Testing Guard

P-Values & Benjamini–Hochberg FDR (q)

Rule: Demand q < 0.05 When Scanning Many Suspects
Plain English: A p-value asks: "If nothing were going on, how weird is this result?" But if you test 25 suspects, 1 in 20 (5%) looks guilty by coincidence! BH-FDR (q) multiplies your toughest test threshold by the number of suspects (`25×` for rank #1).
How Bad Takes Happen: Testing 40 things quietly, throwing away 38 misses, and publishing the 2 lucky flukes as "p < 0.05!"
Dossier #001: All 6 Pew religion metrics cleared BH-FDR at q < 0.00000001 (8 zeros!).
Weapon 03 · Confounder Executioner

Partial Correlation & d-Separation

Notation: r(X, Y | Z)
Plain English: To test whether X truly drives Y or is just riding Z's coattails, we predict X from Z, predict Y from Z, and correlate only the leftover surprises (residuals). If the leftover link hits 0, X and Y are d-separated.
How Bad Takes Happen: Seeing partisan voting correlate with cultural habits and assuming politics caused it.
Dossier #001: 2016 Trump vote dropped from r = −0.419 to Partial r = +0.016 (p = 0.915 — dead zero).
Weapon 04 · Simultaneous Cross-Exam

Matrix OLS (X'X)⁻¹X'y, t-Stats & VIF

|t| > 2.0 ≈ Real Signal · VIF < 5 = No Twin Clones
Plain English: Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) puts all suspect variables in the courtroom simultaneously and asks: "If every other suspect stays frozen, how much does nudging YOU move the map?" t-stat is Signal ÷ Noise; VIF checks if two predictors are near-identical twins stealing each other's voice.
Auditor Superpower: Toggle predictors on/off in our live browser matrix to try breaking the model yourself!
Dossier #001: 8-factor OLS explains R² = 86.5% (Adj R² = 83.8%) across all 50 states.
Weapon 05 · Reversal Detector

Simpson's Paradox (180° Sign Flips)

Raw Slope (+) → Controlled Slope (−)
Plain English: Sometimes an overall scatter plot slopes UP, yet inside every single subgroup the true relationship slopes DOWN! That happens when two opposing forces are bundled together in raw averages.
How Bad Takes Happen: Confusing expensive coastal land with having high disposable family income.
Dossier #001: Income flipped from raw r = +0.37 (cremation) to −0.48 (p = 0.0007, buys $9k burials!).

Exact 2k Shapley R² Decomposition

2⁸ = 256 Predictor Coalitions Tested
Plain English: When Religion and Out-of-State Transplants overlap, whoever enters a regression first looks bigger. Economist Lloyd Shapley solved this: test every possible arrival order (`256` combinations for 8 variables!) and average each variable's exact boost to R².
Why It Matters: Order-independent, mathematically fair credit allocation that sums to 100% of R².

Weapon 07 · Space & Time Forensics

Spatial Moran's I (Map-Ghost Hunter) & Piecewise Structural Breaks

9,999 Random Map Permutations + Δ pp/yr Regime Slopes
Plain English (Space): Neighboring states share history and borders, which inflates ordinary stats. Moran's I checks if our model's leftover errors still clump together on the map compared to 9,999 randomly shuffled maps. If leftover clumping vanishes (`p > 0.05`), no major regional ghost was missed!
